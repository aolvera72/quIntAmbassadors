About The Program

Support, Guidance, and Friendship From Day One.

The Queen's International Student Ambassadors are a diverse, energetic group of undergraduate student leaders who have walked in your shoes. Rigorously trained in peer support, transition guidance, and intercultural communication, our ambassadors bridge the gap between arriving in Kingston and thriving at university.

Whether you have questions about adjusting to Canadian winters, finding the best study spots on campus, understanding your academic program, or getting involved in student life, we are here to provide authentic advice and a friendly face.
